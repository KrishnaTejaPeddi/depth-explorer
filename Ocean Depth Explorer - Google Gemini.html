Epipelagic Zone

Sunlight Zone • 0 – 200m

Enough sunlight penetrates here for photosynthesis. Contains 90% of all marine life despite making up only a tiny fraction of total ocean volume.

Temperature: ~20°C | Light: 100% – 1%

Mesopelagic Zone

Twilight Zone • 200 – 1,000m

Faint light filters down, but no plants can grow. Creatures here rely on keen vision, camouflage, and bioluminescence to survive and hunt.

Pressure: 20 – 100 atm | Light: < 1%

Bathypelagic Zone

Midnight Zone • 1,000 – 4,000m

Complete darkness except for biological light. Water pressure is immense, temperature drops to near freezing, and creatures evolve bizarre adaptations.

Pressure: 100 – 400 atm | Temp: 4°C – 2°C

Abyssopelagic Zone

Abyssal Zone • 4,000 – 6,000m

Covers 83% of the total ocean area. The water here is near freezing, near motionless, and under extreme pressure. Marine snow provides primary nutrients.

Pressure: 400 – 600 atm | Covers most ocean floor

Hadalpelagic Zone

Hadal Zone • 6,000 – 10,935m

Deep oceanic trenches. Named after Hades, god of the underworld. Extremely hostile conditions, yet specialized life thrives near hydrothermal vents.

Max Depth: 10,935m (Challenger Deep) | Pressure: up to 1,086 atm
